Frequently asked questions

Is New.Vision really free?

Yes. All tools and guides are free and require no account. The site is supported by advertising, sponsors and voluntary donations.

Where is my vision board saved?

Only in your own browser (localStorage). We don't upload your photos or goals. Export a PNG to keep a copy or move devices.

Do vision boards actually work?

On their own, imagining success isn't enough. Paired with specific goals and if-then plans, they're a powerful focus tool. Read what the research says.

Can I use my vision board commercially or print it?

Absolutely — it's yours. If you upload photos, make sure you have the right to use them.

How do I get coaching?

Fill in the short form on the coaching page. We'll reply within two business days with options that fit your goal and budget.
