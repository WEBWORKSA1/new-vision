120 Vision Board Ideas for the New Year (By Life Area)

By the New.Vision editorial teamUpdated November 16, 20267 min read

Over 100 specific, inspiring vision board ideas for career, money, health, relationships, travel, home and personal growth — plus words and themes to tie it together.

Key takeaways
  • Pick ideas that are specific and personal — not generic aspirations.
  • Mix 'have', 'do' and 'become' goals for a balanced board.
  • Choose one theme word for the year to tie the board together.

Frequently asked questions

What should I not put on a vision board?

Goals you don't really want, goals that belong to someone else, and anything so vague you couldn't tell if you'd achieved it.

Can couples make a shared vision board?

Yes — make individual boards first, then a shared one with the goals you both care about. It's a great conversation starter.
