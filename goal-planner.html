Why SMART + WOOP?

SMART goals bring clarity: specific, measurable, achievable, relevant and time-bound. WOOP — Wish, Outcome, Obstacle, Plan — adds the piece most planners miss: a pre-decided response to the obstacle that usually derails you. If-then plans like these are among the best-supported tools in motivation research. Compare the frameworks →

Frequently asked questions

How many goals should I set?

Three to five active goals is plenty. Park the rest in a 'later' list.

What does the calendar file do?

It adds four check-in reminders, spaced evenly to your deadline, to Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook.

Can a coach help me with this goal?

Yes — use the 'Get a coach' button on your plan to request a free strategy session.
