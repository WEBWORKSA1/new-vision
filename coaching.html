Coaching FAQ

Is the strategy session really free?

Yes. It's a no-obligation conversation to clarify your goal and options.

Who are the coaches?

Independent coaches and programs we've reviewed. New.Vision may receive a referral fee from some partners; this never changes the price you pay.

Is coaching the same as therapy?

No. Coaching is forward-focused and is not a substitute for mental-health care. If you're in crisis, please contact local emergency services or a licensed professional.

What does it cost after the free session?

It depends on the option you choose — we'll share clear pricing, including free and low-cost options, before you commit.
