Privacy Policy

Last updated November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how New.Vision ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects information when you use https://new.vision (the "Site").

1. Information you give us

When you submit a form (newsletter, workbook, coaching request, contest entry, sponsorship, donation pledge, careers or contact), we receive the details you enter, such as your name, email address and message. Form submissions are delivered to us through a third-party form-processing service (FormSubmit), which processes the data on our behalf.

2. Information stored only on your device

Our tools (vision board, goal planner, Wheel of Life, affirmations and letter to future self) store your content in your browser's local storage. This data is not sent to us. You can delete it at any time by clearing your browser data.

3. Cookies and advertising

We use Google AdSense to display ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this Site and/or other sites on the Internet.

  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland will be asked for consent through a Google-certified consent management platform where required.

4. Embedded content

Videos are embedded using YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode and load only when you press play. Once played, YouTube may set cookies under Google's privacy policy. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

5. How we use information

  • To reply to your messages and requests.
  • To send the workbook and newsletters you asked for (you can unsubscribe at any time).
  • To match coaching requests with suitable partners — only with your consent.
  • To administer contests and process support pledges.
  • To keep the Site secure and improve it.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the Site, with matched partners when you request it, or where required by law.

7. Retention

We keep form submissions only as long as needed for the purpose they were collected, or as required by law.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR, UK GDPR, PIPEDA, CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict use of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. Contact us through the contact form to make a request.

9. Children

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and contests are open only to adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The "last updated" date shows when it was last changed.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy? Use our contact form.
