How to use the vision board maker

  1. Pick a template or start blank. Templates cover a balanced year, career, health, money, travel and family.
  2. Choose 6, 9 or 12 tiles. Fewer, sharper goals beat a crowded collage.
  3. Click a tile to add your own photo, a gradient, an icon and a short, specific caption.
  4. Drag tiles to put your most important goal where your eye lands first.
  5. Download the PNG and set it as your wallpaper, or print it.

Is it private?

Yes. Photos are resized and stored only in your browser's local storage. Nothing is uploaded to our servers. Clearing your browser data will delete the board, so download a PNG to keep it.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use my own photos?

Yes. Upload any image; it's resized on your device for speed and never leaves your browser.

What size is the downloaded image?

Around 1,400 × 1,700 pixels for a 9-tile board — sharp enough to print at A4/Letter or use as a wallpaper.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. Tap a tile to edit. Drag-to-rearrange works best on desktop.
