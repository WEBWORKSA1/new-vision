The New.Vision Workbook

Print this page (or save as PDF) and give yourself one quiet hour. Review the year, design the next one, and leave with a 90-day plan.

Welcome! Your workbook is below — bookmark this page. We'll also send occasional vision prompts to your inbox.

Part 1 — Review this year

  1. What were my three biggest wins this year?
  2. What did I learn the hard way?
  3. What drained my energy most?
  4. Who supported me, and have I thanked them?
  5. What am I ready to let go of?
  6. If this year had a title, what would it be?
