Write a Letter to Your Future Self

Write it today. Choose when it arrives. We create a calendar reminder that carries your letter — so it lands on the exact date, privately, with no account.

0 words

Your letter is never uploaded. You'll download a calendar file (.ics) containing the letter plus a text copy. Open the .ics to add it to Google, Apple or Outlook calendar.

Sealed. 💌

Open the downloaded calendar file to schedule delivery. While you're in a reflective mood, why not turn the letter into a plan?
